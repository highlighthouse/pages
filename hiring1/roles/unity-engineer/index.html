Unity
Engineer

  • Tel Aviv

01 - The company

Highlight is an AI-first mobile gaming studio, building the next generation of mobile games. Games that couldn't be built before AI, where creation is the gameplay itself.

We're looking for a Unity Engineer to build the bridge between our AI systems and the player: turning generative pipelines into responsive, delightful, playable mobile experiences. You'll own the Unity client end to end, from core architecture to the fine details of how AI-generated content feels in the hands of a real player.

02 - The role

Make it
feel right

As Unity Engineer you'll design and build the game client from the ground up inside Unity. You'll work directly with the founders to integrate real-time AI outputs into seamless, performant gameplay, making something that has never existed before feel completely natural to play.

The hard part isn't the engine work on its own. It's that the content is generated live, so no two sessions look the same, and the client has to stay fast and feel intentional anyway. That takes deep Unity expertise and a product sensibility at once: someone who cares not just that it works, but that it feels right.

03 - Responsibilities

What you'll own

  • Architect a scalable, maintainable Unity client built to handle dynamic, AI-generated content in real time.
  • Integrate image and video generation pipelines directly into the game loop with minimal latency impact.
  • Build complex UI systems that adapt to AI output. No two sessions will look the same.
  • Balance performance, memory, and latency in a mobile environment with uncompromising quality standards.
  • Own the client end to end: architecture, build, release, and the quality of what ships.
  • Shape how AI-generated content is presented to the player, together with the founders. The technical and the creative call are the same call here.
04 - Ideal profile

Who you are

  • Deep, hands-on Unity experience. You know the engine inside out, including its limits.
  • Strong C# engineering with a focus on clean architecture and maintainable code.
  • Proven ability to build performant mobile experiences (iOS and/or Android).
  • A sharp eye for feel. You notice when something is 10ms too slow or one animation frame off.
  • Self-directed and comfortable building from zero with limited specification.
05 - Nice to have

Bonus points

  • Experience with real-time multiplayer systems.
  • Background in dynamic UI systems or procedural content.
  • Familiarity with shader programming or GPU optimization on mobile.
  • Experience shipping a consumer mobile game end to end.
★  One more thing
We're building games that only exist because of AI, and the Unity engineer shapes how that actually feels to play. A genuine love of games, and an obsession with what makes them feel great, is not optional.
