01 - The company

Highlight is an AI-first mobile gaming studio, building the next generation of mobile games. Games that couldn't be built before AI, where creation is the gameplay itself.

We're looking for a Full Stack Engineer who can take a feature from an idea to a working experience in players' hands. You'll work directly with the founders to shape the solution and build across the client, backend, data, and AI services needed to deliver it.

02 - The role

From idea to
players' hands

You own making those pieces work together. When a dependency gets in the way, you investigate it, involve the right people, and drive it to resolution. You stay with the feature through release and learn from how players use it.

03 - Responsibilities

What you'll own

  • Turn product ideas into clear, practical implementation decisions, choosing a useful first version and improving it through feedback.
  • Make AI-powered interactions feel responsive and reliable, accounting for generation time, unpredictable results, failures, and cost.
  • Test, release, and monitor your work, using analytics and player feedback to understand whether it delivers the intended experience.
  • Develop quickly while keeping code understandable and features scalable.
04 - Ideal profile

Who you are

You've shipped production features. You're comfortable building interfaces, designing APIs, working with databases, and debugging problems across system boundaries. You can work from an incomplete brief, ask useful questions, and make sound tradeoffs between speed, quality, and scope.

  • Team player. You bring people into decisions early, share context, welcome feedback, and help teammates get unstuck. You take responsibility for delivery while making your work easy for others to understand and contribute to.
  • AI savvy. You use AI tools to accelerate development and verify what they produce. You keep exploring better ways to use AI in development and share what you learn with teammates through reusable skills, agents, and workflows.
  • Game-engine experience. Hands-on experience with Unity or another game engine is a strong advantage. Our game client uses Unity, so you should be ready to learn it and contribute there.
  • B2C experience. Experience shipping consumer products is a strong advantage, especially work improving onboarding, engagement, or retention.
★  One more thing
You care about what the player experiences: whether a creation feels personal, whether a wait feels frustrating, and whether a feature makes someone want to play again.
